A little about me

I am a computer science researcher and educator with experience in postdoctoral research, university teaching, and software development. My research at the University at Buffalo contributes to NSF- and Google-funded projects on handwriting analysis, learning-disability screening, and educational progress monitoring.

Previously, I was an Assistant Professor at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, where I also earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science. My broader work spans computer vision, multimodal learning, healthcare AI, and optimization.
