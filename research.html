01HANDWRITING & EDUCATION

Handwriting & educational AI

My work connects faithful handwriting recognition with educational assessment, early screening, and personalized support for students with writing disabilities.

NSF-FUNDED PROJECT · NATIONAL AI INSTITUTE FOR EXCEPTIONAL EDUCATION

Child handwriting & learning-disability screening

Developing deep-learning and document-analysis methods to recognize and analyze child handwriting in support of early screening for dyslexia and dysgraphia. My work includes faithful and intended-text transcription, explicit handling of spelling errors and character reversals, and reproducible vision-language model training and inference pipelines.

  • Transformers
  • Vision-language models
  • LoRA / QLoRA

Progress monitoring & personalized IEP goals

Developing an AI toolkit for students with writing disabilities. The toolkit analyzes scanned and tablet-based handwriting, combining handwriting measures with text analysis to support progress monitoring, growth charts, personalized IEP goals, and editable reports for educators.

03HEALTHCARE AI

Medical image analysis

Stroke assessment

Developing deep-learning methods for ischemic-stroke assessment using non-contrast CT images. This research explores generative approaches to perfusion-related maps and transformer-guided segmentation of infarct-core and affected brain regions.

  • Medical imaging
  • Generative models
  • Segmentation
